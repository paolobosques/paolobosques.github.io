This project built a genetic algorithm to find optimal pacing strategies for cyclists. Race dynamics are described by an ODE model of rider motion coupled to the Skiba energy-store model, which tracks the depletion and recovery of anaerobic work capacity and acts as the physiological constraint on power output. Rider profiles were fitted to real Strava power data, and strategies were optimized and compared for three cyclists across three courses. Sensitivity analyses on wind speed and on deviation from target power quantified how robust the optimal strategies are to real-world conditions.
