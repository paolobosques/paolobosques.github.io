Computational fluid dynamics models of arterial stents were built in COMSOL Multiphysics at porosities from 70% to 90% and placed in three distinct aneurysm geometries. For each configuration, stent drag force and aneurysm wall pressure were quantified to characterize how porosity and vessel shape jointly govern flow diversion. An 80% porosity emerged as the best compromise, delivering substantial pressure reduction in the aneurysm sac without the drag penalty of denser designs.
