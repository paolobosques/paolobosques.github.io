This project redesigned the chamber and fluid outlet used to test brain–computer interface electrode chips under flow. Chamber geometry was modeled in Blender, flow fields were simulated in COMSOL Multiphysics, and results were post-processed in MATLAB to evaluate candidate designs. The final design reduced the mean flow velocity at the chip surface by 90% compared with the previous chamber, creating conditions gentle enough for fluid-interaction testing over the optrode to proceed.
