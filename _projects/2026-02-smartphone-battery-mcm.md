Over the 100-hour COMAP Mathematical Contest in Modeling, our three-person team developed a state-space model of smartphone battery drain in MATLAB. The model combines Coulomb counting for charge bookkeeping, a second-order equivalent-circuit representation of the lithium-ion cell, and a Kalman filter to estimate state of charge from noisy measurements. It predicts time-to-empty under a range of component-level usage profiles and was validated against NASA lithium-ion cycling data. I co-authored the resulting 25-page technical paper.
